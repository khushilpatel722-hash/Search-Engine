What Privata does

Private by design

No cloud upload, no external search API. Documents and search history stay in this project's data folder.

Fast inverted index

Queries look up words in a dictionary instead of scanning every file, so results arrive in milliseconds.

TF-IDF ranking

Rare, meaningful words count more than common ones, and the best match is shown first with a relevance score.

Snippets and highlights

Every result shows the passage that matches your words, with safe HTML escaping.

Drag-and-drop library

Drop files to index them instantly. Remove or re-index any time, and clear the index without touching your files.

Real analytics

Search count, average search time and top terms are measured from your own activity, never invented.
